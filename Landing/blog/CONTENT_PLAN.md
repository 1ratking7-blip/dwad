## How to use this list
1. Pick a topic, write it as a real article following `blog/README.md`'s "how to add a
   post" steps (own `index.html`, full meta/OG/Twitter/Article schema, `vite.config.ts`
   entry, `sitemap.xml` entry, ref link with a unique `subId2` for attribution).
2. Internally link it to 2–3 already-published posts in the same category, plus one
   cross-category link (e.g. a Provably Fair post linking to a Crypto Basics post) —
   Google credits sites more for a real link graph than isolated pages.
3. Prioritize by category order below — categories are ordered roughly by how directly
   they support conversion (bonus/game topics first) vs. how much they build long-term
   authority and personal brand (football/esports/AI later, but not less valuable — they're
   what makes ZHELEZO look like a real person's project instead of a generic affiliate mill).
